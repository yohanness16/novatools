Your Files Never Touch a Cloud Server

Traditional web tools upload your sensitive documents to remote servers, process them, and force you to download the result. This exposes your data to third-party risks, network latency, and server downtime.

NovaTools operates differently. By leveraging WebAssembly and modern browser APIs, we bring the processing engine directly to your machine. No uploads. No limits. Total privacy.
