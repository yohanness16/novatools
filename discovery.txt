1. EXECUTIVE PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

NovaTools is an industrial-grade, privacy-first web productivity suite that delivers
instant document, image, video, audio, and architectural diagram transformations directly
inside the user's browser. 

Unlike traditional online conversion websites (which upload sensitive personal documents,
legal contracts, medical scans, or audio recordings to remote cloud servers), NovaTools 
performs 100% of all processing locally in volatile browser memory.

KEY VALUE PROPOSITIONS:
1. Absolute Data Sovereignty & Privacy: Zero network transmission of file binaries.
   Files exist strictly in local RAM and vanish on tab close.
2. Zero Latency / Native Speeds: Eliminates multi-gigabyte upload and download waiting
   times by utilizing client CPU/GPU threads directly.
3. Uncapped & Free: No subscription paywalls, no artificial file-size caps, and no daily
   conversion limits.
4. Offline Capable: Can execute on airplanes, offline workstations, or air-gapped secure labs.
5. Modern "Console/Workbench" Aesthetic: High-density, professional UI styled after
   Linear, Vercel, and enterprise cloud consoles (AWS/Azure/GCP) with signature privacy
   ledger strips and real-time visual workbenches.
